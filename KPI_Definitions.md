# KPI Definitions

This document defines the key KPIs and analytical metrics used in the E-Commerce Profitability & Operations Analytics Power BI project.

## Executive KPIs

### Total Revenue

Total sales revenue generated from order items.

**DAX:** Total Revenue = SUMX(FactOrderItems, FactOrderItems[quantity] * FactOrderItems[unit_price])

**Calculation:** Revenue = Quantity × Unit Price

**Validated Value:** ₹1.286B

### Total Cost

Total product cost associated with order items.

**DAX:** Total Cost = SUMX(FactOrderItems, FactOrderItems[quantity] * RELATED(DimProduct[cost_price]))

**Calculation:** Cost = Quantity × Product Cost Price

**Validated Value:** ₹958.123M

### Total Profit

Profit generated after subtracting product cost from revenue.

**DAX:** Total Profit = [Total Revenue] - [Total Cost]

**Calculation:** Profit = Total Revenue − Total Cost

**Validated Value:** ₹328.183M

### Profit Margin %

Percentage of revenue retained as profit.

**DAX:** Profit Margin % = DIVIDE([Total Profit], [Total Revenue], 0)

**Calculation:** Profit Margin % = Total Profit ÷ Total Revenue × 100

**Validated Value:** Approximately 25.51%

### Total Orders

Total number of unique orders in the Orders table.

**DAX:** Total Orders = DISTINCTCOUNT(FactOrders[order_id])

**Calculation:** Counts distinct order IDs from the Orders table.

**Validated Value:** 9,000

### Total Customers

Total number of unique customers.

**DAX:** Total Customers = DISTINCTCOUNT(DimCustomer[customer_id])

**Calculation:** Counts distinct customer IDs.

**Validated Value:** 5,000

### Orders with Items

Number of unique orders that have corresponding order-item records.

**DAX:** Orders with Items = DISTINCTCOUNT(FactOrderItems[order_id])

**Calculation:** Counts distinct order IDs available in the Order Items table.

**Validated Value:** 7,342

### Average Order Value

Average revenue generated per order that has corresponding order-item records.

**DAX:** Average Order Value = DIVIDE([Total Revenue], [Orders with Items], 0)

**Calculation:** AOV = Total Revenue ÷ Orders with Items

**Validated Value:** ₹175.20K

### Profit per Order

Average profit generated per order that has corresponding order-item records.

**DAX:** Profit per Order = DIVIDE([Total Profit], [Orders with Items], 0)

**Calculation:** Profit per Order = Total Profit ÷ Orders with Items

**Validated Value:** ₹44.70K

## Order & Operations KPIs

### Total Order Items

Total number of unique order-item records.

**DAX:** Total Order Items = DISTINCTCOUNT(FactOrderItems[order_item_id])

**Purpose:** Used to understand item-level sales volume and support return analysis.

### Orders Without Items

Number of orders in the Orders table that do not have corresponding order-item records.

**Calculation:** Total Orders − Orders with Items

**Validated Value:** 1,658

### Orders Without Items by Status

- Cancelled — 580
- In Transit — 550
- Delivered — 528

## Returns KPIs

### Total Returned Orders

Number of distinct return records used in the Power BI return KPI.

**DAX:** Total Returned Orders = DISTINCTCOUNT(FactReturns[return_id])

**Validated Value:** 1,176

### Return Rate %

Return rate measure used in the Power BI model.

**DAX:** Return Rate = DIVIDE([Total Returned Orders], [Total Order Items], 0)

**Validated Value:** 13.07%

### Category Return Rate

Percentage of orders containing a category that had at least one returned item from that category.

**Category Values:**

- Apparel/Fashion — 11.69%
- Toys — 11.40%
- Food & Beverages — 10.85%
- Beauty/Cosmetics — 10.64%
- Electronics — 10.43%
- Home & Kitchen — 9.88%

### Return Records

Number of return records stored in the Returns table.

**Purpose:** Measures return activity at the return-record level.

### Refund Amount

Total refund amount associated with return records.

**Calculation:** SUM(refund_amount)

## Profitability Metrics

### Category Revenue

Total revenue generated by each product category.

**Calculation:** SUM(Quantity × Unit Price)

### Category Cost

Total product cost associated with each category.

**Calculation:** SUM(Quantity × Cost Price)

### Category Profit

Total profit generated by each product category.

**Calculation:** Category Revenue − Category Cost

### Category Profit Margin %

Percentage of category revenue retained as profit.

**Calculation:** Category Profit ÷ Category Revenue × 100

**Highest Category Margin:** Apparel/Fashion — 26.92%

### Product Revenue

Total revenue generated by an individual product.

**Calculation:** SUM(Quantity × Unit Price)

### Product Cost

Total cost associated with an individual product.

**Calculation:** SUM(Quantity × Cost Price)

### Product Profit

Total profit generated by an individual product.

**Calculation:** Product Revenue − Product Cost

### Product Profit Margin %

Percentage of product revenue retained as profit.

**Calculation:** Product Profit ÷ Product Revenue × 100

## Customer Metrics

### Customer Orders

Number of unique orders associated with a customer.

**Calculation:** COUNT(DISTINCT order_id)

### Customer Revenue

Total revenue generated by a customer.

**Calculation:** SUM(Quantity × Unit Price)

### Customer Profit

Total profit generated by a customer.

**Calculation:** Customer Revenue − Customer Cost

### Customer AOV

Average revenue generated per customer order.

**Calculation:** Customer Revenue ÷ Customer Orders

## Product Return Metrics

### Product Returned Orders

Number of orders associated with returned items for a product.

### Product Return Rate

Return activity measured at the product level.

### Product Refund Amount

Total refund amount associated with returned items for a product.

**Calculation:** SUM(refund_amount)

## Location Metrics

### Customers by City

Number of unique customers in each city.

### Orders by City

Number of unique orders associated with customers from each city.

### Revenue by City

Total revenue generated from customers in each city.

### Profit by City

Total profit generated from customers in each city.

### Average Order Value by City

Average revenue generated per order represented in the city-level order-item data.

**Calculation:** City Revenue ÷ City Orders

## Order & Payment Metrics

### Orders by Order Status

Number of unique orders grouped by order status.

### Revenue by Order Status

Total revenue associated with each order status.

### Orders by Payment Method

Number of orders associated with each payment method.

### Revenue Share %

Percentage of total revenue generated by a specific segment.

**Calculation:** Segment Revenue ÷ Total Revenue × 100

### Revenue Tier

Business classification based on revenue contribution.

**Classification:**

- HIGH
- MEDIUM
- LOW

## Advanced Analytical Metrics

### Category Product Rank

Ranks products within each category according to total revenue.

**SQL:** RANK() OVER(PARTITION BY CATEGORY_NAME ORDER BY TOTAL_REVENUE DESC)

**Purpose:** Identifies the top products within each category.

### Category Performance Status

Categories are classified using profitability and return performance.

**Classification:**

- STRONG
- AVERAGE
- NEEDS ATTENTION

**Factors Used:**

- Profit Margin
- Category Return Rate

## Data Quality Consideration

The dataset contains 9,000 orders but only 7,342 orders with corresponding order-item records.

Therefore:

- Total Orders = 9,000
- Orders with Items = 7,342
- Orders Without Items = 1,658

The missing-item orders include:

- Cancelled = 580
- In Transit = 550
- Delivered = 528

Revenue and profitability are calculated from order-item level data.

Therefore, Average Order Value and Profit per Order use Orders with Items as their denominator.

This data-quality issue was identified during the final MySQL and Power BI validation process.
